## SECTION: ABOUT

#### About

Backend engineer working mostly with Java and Spring Boot. Nearly 3 years in banking technology made it clear how much of the job happens around the code: testing, debugging, production support and close work with other teams.

It started in 2019 with a part-time internship at Universidad de Montemorelos, on systems used every day by students, professors and administrative staff, and that is where backend work first caught my attention. A Front-End internship at OneCard followed, bringing REST APIs, SQL and transaction monitoring into the picture. These days I care about code that is easy to maintain and well tested, and about knowing why a technical decision was made. Next, I'm looking to grow in software architecture and take on more ownership of end-to-end backend solutions.
